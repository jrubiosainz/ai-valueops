    METHOD / TRACEABILITY

    Know what the evidence means.

    Measurements, cost estimates and policy decisions are distinct. Keep their limits visible.

    How the comparison works

    Each synthetic case is evaluated with full context, selected context plus mandatory policy, and a negative strategy without that policy. Treatment order rotates between repetitions; provider caching is not disabled.
